CineTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · Back to CineTrack

CineTrack is a small personal project for tracking and discovering movies, shared with friends. This page explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Username and password accounts: if you create an account with a username and password, we store your username, an optional email address, and your password in scrambled (hashed) form. We never store or see your password in readable form.
  • Google account details (if you sign in with Google): your name, email address, profile picture and Google user ID. They are used only to sign you in and to keep your lists on your account.
  • Your content: the movie lists you create and the statuses, ratings and notes you add to movies.
  • Session cookie: a cookie keeps you signed in. If you use the app without signing in, a guest cookie links your lists to that browser.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, CineTrack creates a separate calendar named "CineTrack" and adds events for the movies you plan to watch. It uses only the limited permission calendar.app.created, which lets the app manage calendars it created itself. It cannot read your other calendars or events. The access token needed to keep this working is stored encrypted, and you can disconnect at any time from the app's settings, which stops all further access.

CineTrack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services involved

  • TMDB (The Movie Database): movie search and details come from TMDB. Search terms you type are sent to TMDB to fetch results. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

How your information is used and shared

Your information is used only to run the app for you. It is not sold, rented or shared for advertising. It is not shared with anyone except the services above, as needed to provide the features you use.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on a private server in Hyderabad, India. Access is limited to the person who runs the app.

Deleting your data

You can remove individual movies and lists inside the app. To have your whole account and all its data deleted, email the address below and it will be done.
